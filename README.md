# Financial Modeling & Business Intelligence Portfolio

A collection of financial models, corporate valuation frameworks, retail performance analytics, and accounting schedules built in Microsoft Excel. This portfolio demonstrates end-to-end data modeling, CX auditing, profitability analysis, and corporate finance decision frameworks.

## 📌 Projects Overview

### 1. Customer & Product Profitability Analysis
* **Tech Stack:** Excel | Financial Analysis | Pivot Tables | Data Modeling
* **Overview:** An end-to-end financial analysis evaluating customer purchase behavior, product performance, and gross margins across retail transactions. 
* **Key Features:** Features automated summary dashboards, profitability segmentation, and margin analysis to uncover revenue drivers and cost bottlenecks.


### 2. Financial Analysis & Capital Budgeting Model
* **Tech Stack:** Excel | Financial Modeling | Corporate Finance | Valuation
* **Overview:** A corporate financial model covering Time Value of Money (TVM), loan amortization schedules, and capital budgeting decision models.
* **Key Features:** Compares investment projects using Net Present Value (NPV), irregular cash flow XNPV, standard IRR, exact-date XIRR, and Modified IRR (MIRR), alongside monthly EMI splits.

### 3. Retail CX & Advisor Performance Grading
* **Tech Stack:** Excel | Customer Experience (CX) | Store Audit | Data Visualization
* **Overview:** A Business Intelligence audit analyzing retail customer experience (CX) and advisor performance across store zones.
* **Key Features:** Evaluates store ambiance, trial experience, objection handling, and sales closure to grade performance (High, Average, Low) and guide operational improvements.


### 4. Comparative Analysis of Depreciation Methods
* **Tech Stack:** Excel | Accounting & Finance | Financial Reporting | Asset Management
* **Overview:** A dynamic accounting model built to calculate and compare asset depreciation across standard methodologies.
* **Key Features:** Includes side-by-side schedule comparisons (Straight-Line, Written Down Value, etc.) to support financial reporting, tax planning, and asset lifecycle management.
